Equipment and method

Our process begins with inspection. Using a camera, we look inside your ducts to see the real level of dust, debris, moisture or damage. That inspection gives us an honest starting point and gives you a clear before picture.

Next we create negative pressure in the duct system with powerful, sealed collection equipment. Agitation tools loosen buildup from the walls of each duct, and the airflow carries it away into the collector instead of letting it drift into your rooms. We then clean registers, grilles and accessible HVAC components, finish with an after inspection and share photos so you can compare the results.

The same careful method applies to dryer vent cleaning. We use specialized brushes and air tools to clear lint along the full length of the dryer duct, check the exterior termination and confirm that air flows freely before we leave.

How often should I schedule air duct cleaning?

Most homes benefit from duct cleaning every three to five years. Homes with pets, smokers, allergy sufferers or recent renovations may need it sooner.

How long does air duct cleaning take?

A typical home takes two to four hours depending on the number of vents, the size of the system and how much buildup is present.

How often should I clean my dryer vent?

At least once a year. Households that dry heavy loads or run the dryer daily should consider a dryer vent cleaning service twice a year.

Evaporator Coil and Blower Cleaning

The evaporator coil sits inside your air handler and removes heat and moisture from the air. When dust and grime coat the coil, it cannot cool efficiently, airflow drops and the system runs longer to reach your set temperature. Evaporator coils should be cleaned at least once a year.

The blower fan pushes conditioned air through your ducts, and a dirty blower wheel moves less air while spreading dust through the system. Cleaning it restores airflow and protects the motor from extra strain.

Our technicians clean the coil and blower with safe, effective methods and show you the before and after condition. Many customers schedule this service together with duct cleaning so the entire air path is clean from the equipment to the vents, which supports comfort, efficiency and longer equipment life.

Condenser Coil Cleaning

The condenser coil is part of your outdoor air conditioning unit, and it releases the heat your system removes from your home. Leaves, pollen, cottonwood and dirt collect on the coil fins throughout the year.

Because dirty condenser coils make it difficult for your air conditioner to cool your home, the system has to work harder and longer to compensate. That can lead to higher energy bills, uneven cooling and extra wear on the compressor.

Our condenser coil cleaning removes outdoor buildup with proper coil safe cleaners and careful rinsing, so heat can escape freely again. We also check the surrounding area for debris and vegetation that may be blocking airflow. A spring cleaning before the cooling season is a smart way to prepare your system for Trenton summers.
